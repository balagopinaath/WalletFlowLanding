The flaw we fix

A tracker that can't tell you what you have isn't a ledger — it's a diary.

✕ Typical expense app

Records the spend. Forgets the source.

You log ₹412 on dinner and ₹1,200 on groceries. The app tallies "you spent ₹1,612" — but it never subtracted that from your bank, your cash, or anywhere. Ask "how much do I have left?" and it has no honest answer.

✓ WalletFlow ledger

Every rupee lives in a real account.

An expense reduces its account; income increases it. A transfer just moves money between two — and never pretends to be income. Balances are derived from transactions in SQL, never edited by hand.

The promise

No account. No login.
No network. Ever.

Every rupee lives on the device in Room + DataStore. There is no server to leak, no analytics to opt out of, no email to remember. WalletFlow works in a tunnel, on a flight, in a basement — and the balance is still exact, because money is stored as integer paise, never floats.

📴

Offline-first

The database is the source of truth — not a sync queue. Fully local, fully yours, on Android 9.0+ (SDK 28 → 37).

🧮

Exact by design

Integer minor-unit math end to end. ₹20,000 minus three expenses is exactly ₹19,151.00 — the test suite says so.

🔏

Yours to take

CSV or .xlsx export any time, restore on a new device. No lock-in, no subscription wall, no dark patterns.
